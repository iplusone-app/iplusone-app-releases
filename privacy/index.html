Privacy Policy

Applies to the GetiPlusOne desktop application (Windows) and this website. Effective date: September 16, 2026.

1. Summary

GetiPlusOne is an offline-first language learning application. Your dictionaries, word books, flashcards, review history, media and reading progress are stored on your own computer. An account is optional: without signing in, the app works fully offline. There are no ads and no third-party analytics/tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored locally on your device

  • Dictionaries you import, word books, flashcards, review records and study statistics;
  • Imported audio/video/e-book files, generated peaks and thumbnails;
  • Application settings (e.g. theme, language, playback preferences);
  • Downloaded pronunciation models/audio packs.

These files live in the application data folder (e.g. %APPDATA%\englis-master, or the app's container folder when installed from Microsoft Store). Uninstalling the app or deleting that folder removes them.

3. Account and optional cloud sync

If you choose to create/sign in an account (email + password), we use Supabase as the authentication and database provider. In that case the following may be transmitted to and stored on Supabase:

  • your email address and a hashed password (handled by Supabase Auth);
  • your subscription/entitlement status and the subscription identifier issued by the payment provider;
  • learning data you choose to sync (for example word books, flashcards, review progress), so the same account can be used on another device.

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/WSS). We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Payments

  • Website purchases are processed by Paddle as merchant of record. Your card details are entered on Paddle's checkout and are never received or stored by us.
  • Microsoft Store purchases are processed by Microsoft. We only query (through Microsoft's Store services) whether a subscription is active for your account — we never receive your payment details.

5. AI grammar analysis (optional, membership feature)

When you use sentence grammar analysis, the text of the selected sentence (and your interface language) is sent to an AI service to produce the analysis. Depending on your configuration this may be the provider you configure yourself (a "bring your own API key" endpoint, e.g. DeepSeek or another OpenAI-compatible service) or a service we operate. Results are cached on your device. No account credentials, files or dictionaries are sent with that request.

6. Network requests

  • Downloading the optional pronunciation model/audio library from GitHub Releases;
  • Checking for and downloading application updates (the Microsoft Store build is updated by the Store);
  • Sign-in and cloud sync (only if you use an account);
  • AI analysis (only when you invoke it).

As with any internet request, the remote server may see technical information such as your IP address and user agent. We do not build profiles from it.

7. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad SDKs;
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • No access to your camera or contacts;
  • No microphone access unless you actively start recording in the Shadowing (read-aloud) feature — see section 9;
  • No selling or renting of personal data.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it (or uninstall). Account data remains while your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and associated cloud data at any time by emailing us; we will delete it within 30 days, except where we must retain payment records for legal/tax reasons.

9. Microphone and shadowing recordings

The app requests microphone access only when you use the Shadowing (read-aloud) feature in the player and press “Start recording”.

  • The recording is kept in your device’s memory only, so that you can replay it immediately and compare it with the reference audio, and so that the app can count your practice time;
  • It is never uploaded to our servers or to any third party, and it is not written to disk for long-term storage;
  • Microphone capture stops as soon as you stop recording; starting a new recording or leaving Shadowing mode releases the audio data (only the duration in seconds is recorded for study statistics — no audio is kept);
  • You can revoke microphone access at any time in Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone; other features keep working.

10. Children and minors

The app is a general-audience English learning tool and is not directed to children (an age rating only describes content suitability, not the intended audience).

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children;
  • Minors should use the app with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian. Some regions have stricter rules: in Korea users under 14, in the EEA users under 16 (or the age set by each member state), and minors in Japan, Taiwan and other jurisdictions need consent from their legal representative (parent or guardian), or the account should be created by them;
  • If you are a parent or guardian and find that a child has registered or provided personal information without your consent, email us at support@getiplusone.app and we will delete the account and associated data within 30 days;
  • We never use any user data (including that of minors) for advertising or profiling — the app contains no advertising.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced on this page and, where appropriate, in the application. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@getiplusone.app · Subscription Terms & EULA
